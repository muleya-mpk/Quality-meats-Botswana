Our Story

Vertically integrated,
since 1998

Quality Meat (Pty) Ltd has been part of Botswana's meat industry since 1998. As a vertically integrated business, we handle the full chain ourselves — from sourcing livestock through to wholesale, retail and processed meat — so quality stays consistent at every step.

We source primarily from Botswana's communal and commercial farms, supporting local producers directly, and sell both at our Gaborone factory shop and through supermarkets around the country.

Food Safety Certified
